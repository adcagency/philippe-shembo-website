Building with a Healed Heart: The Foundation of Fruitful Service

By Apostle Philippe Andy Shembo • Published on September 20, 2026 • 1 min read

Serving God and fulfilling our destiny cannot rest on fragile foundations or unhealed wounds. All too often, zeal and talent conceal inner fractures that ultimately compromise the entire house.

“Above all else, guard your heart, for everything you do flows from it.” — Proverbs 4:23

1. The Temptation to Serve as Compensation

Throughout pastoral ministry, we encounter many believers driven by great passion. However, when service becomes a search for human validation or an escape from past traumas, it inevitably results in burnout and bitter disappointment.

As explored in the collection “The Healing of Broken Hearts”, God is primarily attentive to the state of the laborer before focusing on the magnitude of the harvest.

2. The Divine Path of Restoration

Heart restoration is not a momentary occurrence, but an intentional journey of truth and grace:

  1. Clear Confrontation: Choosing to face past rejections, wounds, and grief honestly.
  2. Liberating Forgiveness: Relinquishing the desire for retaliation so Christ's peace can flood the soul.
  3. Rooted in Heavenly Identity: Knowing who we are in Christ before defining what we accomplish for Him.

3. A Healed Heart Becomes an Overflow for Others

When the Lord restores a broken heart, what was once an emblem of shame becomes an instrument of spiritual authority. We cannot guide others beyond where we have traveled ourselves with God.

May each of us pause today before the altar of grace to let the Holy Spirit minister to the innermost places of our being.
